Rules and control
We want AI to work like one more person in the team, not like a black box.
1 · Rules

Every problem becomes a rule

Problem→New rule
59rules the AI reads before any work
For example
  • ·Use the same names as in Figma.
  • ·Check every component in the browser, next to Figma.
  • ·"Complete" means all its variants.
→
2 · Checks

Every change is checked

  • ✓Compared with Figma in the browser: sizes, colors and all its variants.
  • ✓Accessible contrast for every new color (WCAG AA).
  • ✓Automatic comparison between Figma and code, to find what is not up to date.
If something doesn't match, the component is not finished.
→
3 · People

People always decide

  • !The AI tells us first before it changes the structure of a component.
  • !If it finds a mistake in Figma, it points it out, but it doesn't fix it alone.
  • ✓Nothing new goes to Figma without the team's approval.
This way, the decisions stay with the team.
